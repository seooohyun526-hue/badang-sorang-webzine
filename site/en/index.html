    badang sorang

    Badang Sorang joins two Jeju words, 'badang' (sea) and 'sorang' (love) — an interview webzine that meets Jeju's haenyeo in person and records their stories.
    We make this webzine out of love for the Jeju sea and the haenyeo who have lived by it.
